City sensing today is expensive, sparse and slow to update.

Fixed CCTV and IoT sensor networks only cover the junctions they were bolted to. Everything between them — the actual stretch of road a commuter travels — stays invisible until someone files a complaint.

Municipal teams end up reacting to problems weeks after they appear, while the data needed to catch them early was, quite literally, driving past the whole time.

Four steps, running quietly on every route the fleet already drives.

No detours, no dedicated survey vehicles — the intelligence layer rides along.
01

Sense

A compact camera and sensor unit on each bus watches the road, traffic and surroundings for the length of its normal route.

02

Detect

Onboard edge-AI flags potholes, blocked lanes, unsafe crowding and air-quality spikes on the spot — no cloud round-trip needed to notice.

03

Stream

Only the flagged event and its location are sent over the network, so bandwidth and data cost stay a fraction of raw video streaming.

04

Act

City control rooms see verified, geotagged issues on a live map and route repair or traffic teams before commuters ever complain.

One camera feed, four kinds of city intelligence.

Each detection category runs on the same onboard unit, tuned to what actually matters for city operations.

Road & infrastructure health

Potholes, faded lane markings, broken signage and damaged footpaths, geotagged the moment a bus passes them.

→ PWD work orders

Traffic & congestion analytics

Live density and flow along every route the fleet covers, well beyond what junction cameras alone can see.

→ signal timing

Public safety events

Unsafe crowding, stalled vehicles and blocked emergency lanes flagged for immediate review.

→ control room

Environmental sensing

Onboard air-quality and noise sensors build a street-level pollution map that static stations can't match.

→ pollution board

Civic issue routing

Verified detections feed straight into existing municipal complaint systems, pre-filled and geotagged.

→ 311 / helpline

How it's built

Edge-first by design, so the network stays light and detection stays fast.
ON-BUS

Edge AI unit

Camera + low-power compute runs detection models directly on the vehicle.

UPLINK

4G / 5G sync

Only flagged events and metadata transmit — not raw video.

CLOUD

Aggregation layer

Events are deduplicated, verified and mapped against the city's road graph.

CONSOLE

City ops dashboard

Operators triage, assign and track resolution in one live view.

PUBLIC

Open data API

Anonymised civic data available for planners and citizen apps.
